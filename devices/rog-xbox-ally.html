ROG Xbox Ally · 2025 · RC73YA

ROG Xbox Ally dock matrix

The non-X 2025 Xbox handheld has two USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C ports with DisplayPort and PD 3.0 — and no USB4, according to ASUS. Dock lists that only say “Ally X” are not automatically applied here.

Last checked 19 Nov 2026, Europe/Athens 15 sources checked on this page See a mistake? There is no intake form yet. Keep the source URL and the retrieval date.

Best-supported docks for ROG Xbox Ally

Up to three entries, chosen for the strongest official documentation on this handheld — not a ranking from tests by this site, and not a promise that 4K120 or VRR will appear on your display.

#1 by official documentation

ROG Bulwark Dock DG310

Confirmed by maker
HDMIUSB-CUSB-PD

ASUS’s official series dock. HDMI 2.1 up to 4K at 144 Hz or 8K at 30 Hz, with VRR, HDR and ALLM claimed. Up to 80 W to the host from a 100 W charger, which is not included. The 80 W figure is the dock’s rating; the official handheld adapter is 65 W.

#2 by official documentation

JSAUX 6-in-1 HB0612

Confirmed by maker
HDMIUSB-CUSB-PD

Official page is titled for ROG Xbox Ally X and ROG Xbox Ally. HDMI 2.1 at 4K 120 Hz and PD input up to 100 W are claimed. VRR is not stated on that page. Protective cases are not compatible with these two handhelds in this dock, per JSAUX.

#3 by official documentation

UGREEN 6-in-1 (SKU 75126)

Confirmed by maker
HDMIUSB-CUSB-PD

Official notes list ROG Xbox Ally / Ally X 2025. HDMI 2.1 up to 4K 120 Hz, plus VRR, FreeSync and G-Sync, are claimed. 100 W PD input, up to 85 W to the host. One undated official-site review reported the dock would not power; that report is in the table below.

ROG Bulwark Dock DG310
ASUS
 Designed and optimised for the ROG Xbox Ally series (automatic Game Mode and one-touch Bluetooth). Works over USB-C with any host that supports DisplayPort Alt Mode and USB PD (Windows 11+, macOS 12.3+, Android 14+). The stand is shaped for ROG Xbox Ally (2025), Ally X (2025) and Ally X20 (2026) — not the 2023/2024 Ally body. HDMI 2.1: up to 3840×2160 at 144 Hz, or 7680×4320 at 30 Hz (maker). Actual mode depends on the handheld, cable and display (ASUS FAQ). Yes — VRR, plus HDR and ALLM, claimed on the HDMI 2.1 port (product page and FAQ). Up to 80 W to the host. PD input on the dock up to 100 W (20 V / 5 A). A 100 W or higher USB-C PD charger is required for the full 80 W. Charger not included. Single external display only. The 80 W figure is the dock’s rating; ASUS’s official handheld adapter for both 2025 Ally models is 65 W, and this site has no official maximum intake figure above that for the handhelds.
