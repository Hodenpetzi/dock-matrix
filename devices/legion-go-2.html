Lenovo Legion Go 2 · PSREF 8ASP2

Legion Go 2 dock matrix

Two USB4 ports carry video and 65–100 W charging. Two more USB-C ports on the controllers charge only — a dock there will not give you a picture. This page follows Lenovo’s 8ASP2 PSREF (Z2 Extreme), dated 25 May 2026.

Plug the dock into a USB4 port Lenovo draws four USB-C ports that look similar. Only the two USB4 ports carry DisplayPort. The controller ports are charge-only. If a dock appears “dead” except for charging, the first check is the port, not the brand of dock.

Other Legion Go 2 machine types may exist (for example a Z2 configuration). They are not specified on this page, because their official sheets were not fully read in this build. Do not assume every Go 2 SKU has DisplayPort 2.1.
