MSI Claw 8 AI+ A2VM · Intel Core Ultra (Series 2)

MSI Claw 8 AI+ dock matrix

Two Thunderbolt 4 ports, both with DisplayPort and Power Delivery 3.0, and a 65 W charging ceiling, according to MSI. Below: MSI’s own Nest dock, and third-party docks that name this handheld — or only the “MSI Claw” family.

Best-supported docks for MSI Claw 8 AI+

Up to three entries, chosen for the strongest official documentation on this handheld — not a ranking from tests by this site, and not a promise that 4K120 or VRR will appear on your display.

#1 by official documentation

MSI Nest Docking Station

Confirmed by maker
HDMIUSB-CUSB-PD

MSI’s own dock, with Claw 8 AI+ A2VM named under Compatible Models (re-checked 24 Nov 2026). HDMI 2.0 up to 4K 60 Hz, Gigabit Ethernet, three USB 3.2 Gen 1 ports, PD input up to 100 W. MSI notes the Claw itself charges at up to 65 W. No VRR claim.

#2 by official documentation

JSAUX 6-in-1 HB0612

Confirmed by maker
HDMIUSB-CUSB-PD

Names MSI Claw 8 AI+ by model in its compatibility list. HDMI 2.1 up to 4K 120 Hz HDR and PD input up to 100 W are claimed. VRR is not stated on the page. Base takes handhelds up to 27 mm thick, per JSAUX.

Either Type-C port should carry video Unlike Legion Go 2, MSI lists DisplayPort and Power Delivery on both Thunderbolt 4 ports. A dock that only charges is a dock or cable problem to check first, not a wrong-port problem.
100 W docks, 65 W handheld Many docks here accept 100 W input. MSI states the Claw’s maximum charging speed is 65 W. A 100 W charger is still what MSI recommends for the Nest dock, so the dock’s own ports have power too. That is MSI’s recommendation, not a measurement by this site.

This page covers the Claw 8 AI+ A2VM. Claw A1M, Claw 7 AI+ and Claw A8 are different machines. The user guide cited covers the Claw 7 and Claw 8 (MS-1T42 / MS-1T52); it does not print the “A2VM” name itself. A dock listing for “MSI Claw” is not treated here as a listing for this model. MSI’s Nest list also names Claw A8, Claw 7 AI+ and Claw A1M. The Claw 7 AI+ A2VM (added 25 Nov 2026) and the Claw A8 BZ2EM (added 26 Nov 2026) have their own pages; Claw A1M does not.
