Methodology

How we source

Quality before volume. Every cell should be traceable. If we cannot point to a source, the cell stays empty or is marked Unknown.

Last checked 19 Nov 2026, Europe/Athens 34 sources checked on this page See a mistake? There is no intake form yet. Keep the source URL and the retrieval date.

Order of evidence

  1. Official manufacturer publications — specification pages, PSREF sheets, press-release specification tables, official FAQs, and official support posts published under the maker’s name (including a named support account on X).
  2. Dated user reports — only when a public thread or post is dated and is relevant to a named SKU or a clearly described dock. These never override a maker statement silently; they sit beside it.
  3. This site’s own tests — none, in this build. We will not phrase any cell as if we measured it.

Status badges

Confirmed by maker Maker states compatibility (or incompatibility) for this device. Reported by users Dated user or support report; not a test by this site. Conflicting Official sources disagree, or one official page disagrees with itself. Unknown No clear official statement for this device, or the official page could not be read.
  • Confirmed by maker covers both “listed as compatible” and “listed as not compatible.” Read the compatibility column.
  • Reported by users is reserved for rows whose decisive evidence is a dated user or informal report, not the official list. In this first build, most rows are still maker-led; user reports often appear under “Known issues” instead of taking over the badge.
  • Conflicting is used when two official sources disagree, or one official page disagrees with itself.
  • Unknown means we do not have a clear official statement for that device, or an official page could not be read.

Rules we will not break

  • No invented resolution, VRR or wattage figures.
  • A dock’s HDMI ceiling is not the same thing as the handheld’s output on that dock.
  • A listing for “ROG Ally X” is not automatically a listing for “ROG Xbox Ally X (2025).”
  • A listing for “Legion Go” or “Legion Go S” is not automatically a listing for “Legion Go 2.”
  • Marketplace titles (including Amazon) are not used as a substitute for the maker’s own compatibility list.
  • Retrieval dates are shown. Times on this site are in Europe/Athens unless a source carries its own zone and is converted.
  • Product photographs appear only with a documented licence. This build uses original inline SVGs instead. Port icons (HDMI, DisplayPort, USB-C, USB-PD) are also original SVGs.

What this first build could not finish

  • Google Trends was not used on these product pages (it was blocked with HTTP 429 in a prior research pass).
  • Lenovo’s US product page for the Legion Go USB-C Dock still returns “Access Denied.” The dock’s PSREF accessory sheet was read on 23 Nov 2026, Europe/Athens, and the Legion Go 2 row is filled from it and from the datasheet it links.
  • Ayn Thor and OneXPlayer device pages are not in this build. MSI Claw 8 AI+ (20 Nov 2026), MSI Claw 7 AI+ (25 Nov 2026) and MSI Claw A8 (26 Nov 2026, Europe/Athens) have pages; Claw A1M is not covered.
  • JSAUX Support posts on X were archived in a prior research pass on 19 Nov 2026; they were not re-fetched as live pages in the build step.

Corrections

If a maker updates a list or a quoted page changes, the cell should be updated with a new retrieval date. There is no intake form yet. Keep the source URL and the retrieval date so the cell can be corrected.
